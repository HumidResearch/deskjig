DeskJig is open source — and now the app says so.

## What's new

- **Find us on GitHub**: Settings > Help & Feedback now links straight to
  the [DeskJig repository](https://github.com/HumidResearch/deskjig), with a
  note crediting contributors. Star it, file issues, send PRs.
- Under the hood: the CI pipeline was overhauled around Xcode 26's
  compilation cache, so every change ships through faster, more reliable
  test and release lanes.
