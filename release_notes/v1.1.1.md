The workspaces list finally works the way your hands expect — and restores stop being keyboard-only.

## What's new

- **Open a workspace with the mouse** — every workspace card now has an
  **Open** button, and double-clicking the card body restores it too. A single
  click selects the card.
- **Visible keyboard selection** — arrowing through the list now shows an
  unmistakable highlight on the selected row, and Return always launches
  exactly the row that's highlighted (selection tracks the workspace itself,
  not its position, so search filtering can't shift it).

## Fixes

- The first click into a DeskJig window that wasn't frontmost no longer gets
  swallowed by activation — it lands on the control you clicked.
- Restoring a workspace could still crash an app mid-launch in two corner
  cases (two windows of the same app restoring across displays, and windows
  reused from a previous session). All positioning now waits for the target
  app to finish launching, closing out the Apple Notes crash for good.
